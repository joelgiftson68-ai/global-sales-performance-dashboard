Global Sales Performance & Country Growth Dashboard

Project Overview

This portfolio project analyzes global sales performance across countries, regions, product categories, and customer segments. The dashboard is designed to help a sales or business team identify strong markets, weak markets, profitability trends, and geographic expansion opportunities.

Business Problem

A global sales team needs a consolidated view of:

• Which countries generate the most revenue and profit?
• Which markets are growing or declining year over year?
• Which product categories and customer segments perform best?
• Where should marketing and sales resources be increased?
• Which countries require margin, pricing, or cost intervention?

Dashboard KPIs

• Revenue
• Profit
• Profit Margin
• Year-over-Year (YoY) Growth
• Country-level performance
• Product category performance
• Customer segment performance

Key Features

• Country-wise revenue, profit, margin and growth analysis
• Comparison of 2025 vs 2026 country revenue
• Product category and customer segment insights
• Conditional formatting to highlight growth differences
• Visual country growth and revenue comparisons
• Business recommendations for expansion and performance improvement

Key Insights

The demo dataset is intentionally modeled with different country growth profiles. Markets such as India, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kenya and Nigeria represent higher-growth opportunities, while Argentina, South Africa and Italy are used as examples of markets requiring closer margin/growth review.

Business Impact

The dashboard demonstrates how analytics can support geographic prioritization. Decision-makers can use the outputs to direct marketing spend toward high-potential countries, investigate weak markets, and focus on categories or segments that deliver stronger revenue and profitability.
